FAQ

The practical questions
sellers ask first.

Do my photos upload to your server?

No. Processing runs entirely in your browser. Your card photos never leave your device.

Which browsers work best?

Chrome, Edge, and Brave are the preferred path. Safari still works, but exports as a ZIP download rather than writing directly to a folder.

Who is this best for?

It is especially useful for higher-value singles where corner detail builds buyer confidence. It also helps any seller who wants to process a full batch and come away with complete, listing-ready images for every card — without taking extra shots.

Do I really get corner crops without photographing corners separately?

Yes. CardWorks crops all 8 corners directly from the two photos you took for each card — 4 from the front photo, 4 from the back. You never reposition, refocus, or take additional shots. It works this way for every card in your batch.

What happens after I sign up?

You buy access on Gumroad, receive your license key immediately, enter it on the access page, and you are in the workspace. Every card in your first batch gets the complete image set automatically.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Nothing to install. The app runs entirely in your browser, so you open it, upload your photos, and export from there.
