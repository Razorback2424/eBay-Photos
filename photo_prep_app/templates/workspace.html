Tips for sharper exports Expand
Shoot at full resolution

Corner crops are zoomed-in sections of your original photo. Use the native camera at full resolution and avoid screenshot imports or resized images.

Flat card on a plain background

A dark or neutral surface with no strong patterns makes edge detection more reliable, especially in batch shots.

Good light, no flash

Flat window light or a softbox avoids glare on holos and foils, which is exactly what buyers are judging in the corner crops.
